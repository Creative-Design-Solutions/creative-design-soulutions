Notes Vault

I built Notes Vault to have a bank for any passwords and log-ins when I began doing development work.

It holds every password and private note in one encrypted file that lives on your Mac. It uses a passphrase or Apple finger Touch ID to unlock it. You can bring your old passwords in from any CSV, and export them all out whenever you wish. There's a built-in Apple menu bar search as well for super easy access.

Mac app

AYA

Sharing your voice is empowering and transformative. In this digital aging of the times, I wanted a way of keeping that alive. AYA was created for this.

Hold a key. Talk. Let go. Your words come in where you want them — an email, a message, a search box, anywhere you can type.

The speech model runs on your own Mac, on its own graphics chip, so nothing you say is sent anywhere; and there's no monthly fee. No one is storing what you're saying on a server or using it for anything. Choose your own hot key to activate the mic to listen. Hear a small note to know it's active (or turn it off), talk, then let go and it records it into text. Share with AYA the words it keeps mishearing regularly and it evolves. Let it tidy up the ums, or just keep every word, as you are.
